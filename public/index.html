Watch an ε-NFA turn into a DFA, one subset at a time.

Automata Studio is the CSET302 simulator for converting an ε-NFA to a DFA by the Rabin–Scott subset construction (a plain NFA works too — it's just the case with no ε-moves). Enter your automaton and watch every ε-closure, every move, and the DFA growing beside it — then it checks automatically that both machines accept the same language.

Open the ε-NFA → DFA simulator

What the simulator does

The input accepts text, a transition table or a random example, is checked as you type, and plays back every step at the speed you choose.

ε-closure & move

Iterative ε-closure with cycle termination, and the union move(S, c) for each input symbol, shown explicitly at every step.

Subset construction

The NFA lights up the states in each subset while the DFA grows beside it, and the working table fills in cell by cell with a full step log.

Automatic verification

After conversion the engine runs the NFA and the DFA on every string up to a length bound and confirms they accept exactly the same language.
